Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Mythrovia Studios ("we", "us") operates Mythrovia, an audio-story application for Android and iOS. This policy explains how personal information may be handled when you use the app and how to contact us about your privacy.

Information processed

  • Account and sign-in: Mythrovia supports guest (anonymous) sessions and sign-in through available identity providers such as Google or Apple. When you sign in, account identifiers and information made available by the provider, such as an email address, may be processed.
  • Listening and library: We store information needed to provide your library, playback progress, episode access, and preferences.
  • Coins and unlocks: We process virtual coin balances, episode unlocks, reward requests, and related transaction records to operate app features and prevent duplicate or fraudulent rewards.
  • Technical and advertising information: Advertising partners may process device identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and consent choices as described in their own policies, subject to applicable law and your choices.

Why we use information

We use information to provide and maintain Mythrovia, authenticate users, synchronize listening progress, manage coins and episode access, deliver and verify rewarded advertisements, respond to support requests, secure the service, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Services we use

Mythrovia uses Supabase for backend services and account-related data; supported sign-in providers may include Google and Apple. We use Google AdMob to provide advertisements, including optional rewarded ads. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Advertising and consent

Rewarded advertisements are optional: you choose whether to watch one to request an in-app reward. Where required, Mythrovia requests advertising consent and offers applicable privacy choices. Advertising data processing depends on your region, consent choices, and applicable law. You can contact us for help with privacy choices.

Legal bases and your rights

Where the GDPR applies, processing may rely on performance of a contract (providing app features), legitimate interests (such as security and fraud prevention), consent (where required for advertising technologies), or legal obligations, as appropriate. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of your personal data, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Data retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service and for legitimate operational, security, or legal purposes. Retention periods can vary by data type. To request account or personal-data deletion, contact mythrovia.support@gmail.com. Some information may need to be retained where legally required or necessary to resolve security or transaction issues.

International processing

Our service providers may process data in countries outside your country of residence. Where required, international transfers must be protected using appropriate safeguards under applicable data protection law.

Children

Mythrovia is not intended for children below the applicable minimum age for independent consent to online services. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children contrary to applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us.

Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app and its services evolve. The latest version will be published at this address with an updated date.
