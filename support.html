Contact us

For help with your account, playback, missing episodes, coin purchases, rewarded ads, or other technical issues, email:

mythrovia.support@gmail.com

Please include a short description of the problem, your device type, and any relevant error message. For purchase issues, include the product and approximate purchase date, but never send passwords or payment-card details.

Common support topics

  • Signing in or linking your account
  • Audio playback and listening progress
  • Coins, purchases, and episode unlocks
  • App bugs and technical problems
  • Account or data-related requests
